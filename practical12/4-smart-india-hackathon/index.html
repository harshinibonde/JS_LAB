About SIH

Smart India Hackathon (SIH) is a national-level, open innovation platform started in 2017. It is run by the Ministry of Education's Innovation Cell together with AICTE and partner organisations.

Teams of students pick a problem statement posted by an organisation and build a working prototype within a fixed time, first at college level and then at the national grand finale.

Two editions every year:
Software edition – apps, platforms, AI and data solutions.
Hardware edition – devices, sensors and embedded systems.

Popular themes

  • Smart education – tools for learning and skilling
  • Healthcare & MedTech – access and diagnostics
  • Agriculture & FoodTech – farmers and supply chains
  • Smart automation & robotics
  • Disaster management – early warning and response

Achievements

SIH has grown into one of the world's largest open-innovation programmes for students. Over the years it has:

  • Brought thousands of colleges and lakhs of students into one competition.
  • Produced prototypes that ministries and departments have reviewed for real use.
  • Given winning teams prize money and national recognition.

Exact participation numbers differ by year; check the official site for the latest figures.

Placements & opportunities

SIH is not a recruitment drive, but it helps students stand out. Finalists and winners often gain:

  • Interview shortlists and internship offers from partner companies.
  • Mentorship from industry and government experts.
  • A strong project and teamwork story for their résumé.
